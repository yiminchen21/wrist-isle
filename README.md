# Wrist Isle 腕愈岛

A healing recovery game, designed for TFCC patients.
为 TFCC 患者设计的疗愈康复游戏。

- **Game edition:** https://yiminchen21.github.io/wrist-isle/game.html
  Fullscreen, main menu, combos, stage stars.
- **Web edition:** https://yiminchen21.github.io/wrist-isle/web.html
  Calm, journal-style layout.

Three gentle wrist moves (tendon glide, forearm rotation, wrist flexion / extension), tracked by camera or a hold button. Every finished session plants a tree on the island. Hand tracking (MediaPipe) runs locally in the browser; video is never uploaded.

Wrist Isle supports home exercise and does not replace diagnosis or treatment. The method and references are under "Method & safety" in the game.
